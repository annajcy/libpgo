\begin{frame}{Question and Answer}
  \begin{block}{Main question}
    During a contact-rich dynamic drop, does tricubic Hermite produce a
    visible-surface trajectory closer to a same-domain tet-linear numerical
    reference than cubic-linear FEM?
  \end{block}

  \vspace{0.5em}
  \begin{block}{Result of the complete rerun}
    Yes. Post-contact mean relative surface displacement error is
    \pct{4.34} for original cubic-linear, \pct{1.57} for refined
    cubic-linear, and \good{\pct{0.21}} for tricubic Hermite.
  \end{block}

  \begin{itemize}
    \item At comparable DOF scale, Hermite is \good{7.3x lower-error} than refined cubic-linear after contact.
    \item This gain uses 6.86x the wall time of refined cubic-linear; Hermite still uses 14.44\% less wall time than the tet reference.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Data Source and Validation}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Run metadata: current per-case \case{summary.json} files.
    \item All cases are fresh runs from timestep 0 to 800; none was resumed.
    \item Metrics were recomputed from 80 surface dumps per case.
    \item Every dump has 4,214 vertices and 8,424 faces with matching visible-surface connectivity.
    \item Every run accepted all 800 simulation steps.

\begin{frame}{Surface Trajectory Metric}
  \small
  Let $S$ be the visible surface vertices, $x_i^0$ the rest position,
  $x_i^c(t)$ the position for case $c$, and $x_i^{ref}(t)$ the tet-reference
  position at dumped frame $t$.

  \[
    u_i^c(t)=x_i^c(t)-x_i^0,
    \qquad
    u_i^{ref}(t)=x_i^{ref}(t)-x_i^0
  \]

  \[
    E_c(t)=
    \frac{
      \sqrt{\sum_{i\in S}\|u_i^c(t)-u_i^{ref}(t)\|_2^2}
    }{
      \sqrt{\sum_{i\in S}\|u_i^{ref}(t)\|_2^2}
    }
  \]

  \vspace{0.4em}
  \begin{block}{Interpretation}
    $E_c(t)$ measures visible-surface displacement error relative to the
    same-domain tet baseline, not error at volume mesh nodes.
  \end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item The tet solve is a same-domain numerical baseline, not ground truth.
    \item Three cases contain two accepted steps marked \case{MAX_ITERATIONS}.
    \item Contact timing is sampled only every 10 simulation steps.
    \item \case{cubic_linear_x8} is comparable-DOF, not exactly equal-DOF.
    \item This is one geometry, material, timestep, and contact configuration.
    \item Wall-time conclusions are scoped to this server and 12-thread setup.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conclusion}
  \small
  \begin{block}{Main result}
    In the complete bunny drop rerun, tricubic Hermite produces the closest
    visible-surface trajectory to the same-domain tet-linear reference,
    especially after contact.
  \end{block}

  \vspace{0.6em}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Post-contact mean rel L2: \pct{4.34} $\rightarrow$ \pct{1.57} $\rightarrow$ \good{\pct{0.21}}.
    \item Late mean rel L2: \pct{6.45} $\rightarrow$ \pct{2.29} $\rightarrow$ \good{\pct{0.34}}.
    \item Final rel L2: \pct{12.62} $\rightarrow$ \pct{3.41} $\rightarrow$ \good{\pct{1.10}}.
    \item Hermite vs x8: 6.86x wall time for 7.33x lower post-contact error.
    \item Hermite uses 14.44\% less wall time than the tet reference.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
